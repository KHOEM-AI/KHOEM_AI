## Letter in English

Replace everything in [brackets] before sending.

**Subject:** Request for written permission to use Khmer text for language-model training

Dear [Name / Organization],

I am [Your name], leading KHOEM_AI, a project that builds tools for the Khmer language. Our goal is a Khmer text corpus whose rights are clear, used to improve Khmer language models. We would like your written permission to use text from [publication / website / collection] ([date range, approximate number of texts]).

**What we would do**

- Collect text from [exact source and date range].
- Clean it, remove duplicates and personal details, and use it to train and evaluate Khmer language models.
- Keep the original files private. We will not republish your text.

**What we ask**

1. Permission to use the text for model training and internal analysis (yes / no).
2. Any conditions, such as naming you as a source, excluding some content, or a time limit.
3. Whether we may keep your reply as our license record.

You may ask us to remove your content at any time and we will comply. We will not collect anything until we have your written answer.

Thank you for your time.

[Your name]
[Email or phone]
[Date]
